# O15 MiniMaxH3Director 8151b29 acceptance record

## Goal and environment

The goal is to move official Director from `576424f` to `8151b29` without modifying ComfyUI or Director, retain the dual-V100 FSDP/P2P path, and make the new per-segment LoRA stack execute inside both workers without cross-segment contamination.

- Windows, 2x Tesla V100-SXM2-16GB, TCC, driver 577.00, NVLink/CUDA P2P;
- Python 3.12.10, PyTorch 2.12.0+cu126, Ray 2.57.0, xFormers 0.0.35;
- Raylight 1.17.0 candidate and clean official Director `8151b29`;
- P2P capacity 256 MiB, VRAM reserve 4 GiB, and `--disable-cuda-malloc`;
- raw JSON/log/media evidence remains local under `.local/artifacts/o15/`.

## Upgrade gates

The official suite reports `2 passed, 10 subtests passed`. The clean Director checkout and `origin/main` both resolve to `8151b29`. The live schema registers Director, Refine, SelfLift, FaceRefine, and Semantic Bridge, and exposes `cache_frames_codec`, `cache_name`, `export_pre_face_refine`, and `images_pre_face_refine`.

A direct FFV1 smoke round-tripped five 32x48 RGB uint8 frames pixel-exactly. Custom cache names resolved to distinct `workflow-A_5`, `workflow-B_5`, and `workflow-A_6` keys. The real hardware output preserved its decoded 32 kHz audio rate. The two-segment run completed after the Director prefetch cleanup change with no OOM or actor death.

## Automation and conclusion

- Final full suite: `769 passed, 219 warnings, 35 subtests passed`. Warnings are existing Torch deprecations, NestedTensor prototype notices, and Windows pytest temporary-directory cleanup; there are no test failures.
- Director compatibility set: 96 tests plus 2 subtests passed.
- Hardware gate: both GPUs in TCC; correctness/dtype/tail/async P2P checks pass; median remote bandwidth is about 61.4 GiB/s for the 100 GiB trials.
- Actor-log pairing is anchored to the latest same-rank preparation start instead of a fixed two-second window, including a regression where a missing earlier line must not shift later profiles.

O15 passes. Director `8151b29` first-pass, SelfLift, and same-checkpoint Refine continue through the dual workers, and the new segment LoRA stack is request-scoped. FFV1, cache namespaces, audio metadata, selected-run/export, and prefetch cleanup remain Director main-process features; Raylight validates coexistence rather than claiming acceleration.

Not every FL2V/selected-run/FaceRefine combination was rerun as a long quality matrix in this stage. Their schema and bridge gates pass and prior O13/O14 hardware capabilities are retained. A future Director that changes or bypasses `sample_single_stage` will fail explicitly instead of silently sampling locally.
